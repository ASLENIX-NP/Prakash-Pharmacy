% SECTION 1: POINT-OF-SALE (POS) BILLING TERMINAL

\section{Fast Point-of-Sale (POS) Billing Terminal}

The Point-of-Sale (POS) terminal is the heart of your pharmacy. It is built to make customer checkouts fast, accurate, and completely stress-free during rush hours.

\featurecard{1. Lightning-Fast Drug Search (Brand, Generic \& Barcode)}{Instant Search}{
You can find any medicine in less than a second simply by typing the first few letters of the brand name (e.g., \textit{Amox...}), the generic molecule name (e.g., \textit{Paracetamol}), or scanning its barcode with a handheld scanner.
}

\featurecard{2. Automatic Expiry Prioritization (FEFO)}{Smart Shelf Clearing}{
When multiple batches of the same medicine are in stock, the system automatically selects the batch expiring earliest (\textit{First-Expired, First-Out}). This ensures your oldest stock is dispensed first, preventing medicines from expiring unnoticed on your shelves. Cashiers can also click to pick a different batch if a customer specifically requests it.
}

\featurecard{3. Sell Full Packs or Loose Tablets with Instant Unit Math}{Dual Packaging}{
You never have to manually calculate price-per-tablet using a pocket calculator:
\begin{itemize}
    \item When a customer asks for 1 strip or box, enter the pack quantity.
    \item When a customer asks for loose tablets (e.g., 3 tablets of an antibiotic), enter the tablet quantity.
    \item The system automatically divides the MRP by the pack size (\textit{Units Per Pack}) to give the exact fair price and deducts the correct loose tablets from your inventory.
\end{itemize}
}

\featurecard{4. Multiple Flexible Payment Methods}{All Payment Tenders}{
Customers can pay using whatever method they prefer:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Cash:} Enter cash tendered; the system displays the exact change to return to the customer.
    \item \textbf{Fonepay / QR Code:} Select QR Code to record mobile digital payments.
    \item \textbf{Card:} Record debit or credit card payments.
    \item \textbf{Customer Credit (Udharo):} Bill directly to the customer's digital Khata account.
    \item \textbf{Split Payments:} Customers can pay part in cash and part in Fonepay (e.g., Rs. 500 Cash + Rs. 350 QR).
\end{itemize}
}

\featurecard{5. Instant Line-Item or Bill-Level Discounts}{Easy Discounts}{
Apply a flat discount (e.g., Rs. 20 off) or a percentage discount (e.g., 10\% off) to individual medicines or the entire bill with a single keystroke.
}

\featurecard{6. Professional Nepal Tax Receipts (Thermal 80mm or Full A4/A5)}{Print Ready}{
Print professional receipts immediately upon billing. Formatted with your pharmacy name, address, PAN number (\texttt{123798907}), DDA registration, sequential invoice number, and customized thank-you note. Works seamlessly with standard 80mm thermal receipt printers and standard desktop printers.
}

\benefitbox{
Prevents long customer queues, stops pricing mistakes on loose tablets, ensures your oldest batches sell first, and creates professional tax receipts in seconds.
}

% ==============================================================================
\section{Complete Inventory, Batch \& Expiry Control}

Never lose track of your stock or let medicines expire on your shelves. The inventory system keeps an accurate, real-time count of every single pack and loose tablet in your store.

\featurecard{1. Real-Time Pack \& Loose Unit Tracking}{Accurate Stock}{
The inventory dashboard shows exactly what you have in stock at any moment:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Full Packs:} Number of unopened boxes or strips.
    \item \textbf{Loose Units:} Number of loose tablets, capsules, or ampoules currently in opened packs.
    \item \textbf{Automatic Box Opening:} When you sell loose tablets, the system automatically breaks open a full pack and moves the remaining units into your loose unit count.
\end{itemize}
}

\featurecard{2. Color-Coded Expiry Visualizer}{Zero Waste}{
Your inventory is color-coded so you can spot near-expiry medicines from across the room:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \colorbox{RoseLight}{\textbf{\color{RoseDanger}Expired (Red):}} Date has passed. Selling is blocked automatically to protect patients and keep your pharmacy safe.
    \item \colorbox{AmberLight}{\textbf{\color{AmberWarn}Expiring in $\le$ 30 Days (Orange):}} Urgent alert! Dispense first or return to your distributor for replacement/credit.
    \item \colorbox{AmberLight}{\textbf{\color{AmberWarn}Expiring in $\le$ 90 Days (Yellow):}} Early warning to prioritize these medicines.
    \item \colorbox{EmeraldLight}{\textbf{\color{EmeraldGreen}Safe Stock (Green):}} Healthy stock with plenty of shelf life remaining.
\end{itemize}
}

\featurecard{3. DDA Regulatory Flags (Prescription Required \& Narcotic Drugs)}{Government Compliance}{
The system clearly marks:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Prescription / Rx Required:} Reminds staff to check and keep a doctor's prescription before dispensing.
    \item \textbf{Narcotic / Psychotropic Drugs:} Flags controlled medicines to satisfy Department of Drug Administration (DDA) inspection rules.
\end{itemize}
}

\featurecard{4. Rack and Shelf Location Finder}{Instant Shelf Finding}{
Every medicine can be tagged with its physical location in your shop (e.g., \textit{Rack A-2}, \textit{Fridge}, \textit{Emergency Counter}, \textit{Ointment Shelf 1}). New staff can locate any medicine immediately without having to ask where it is kept.
}

\featurecard{5. Distributor Bonus Goods Tracking (Free / Bonus Packs)}{Free Scheme Goods}{
When a distributor gives you free medicines (such as 10+1 free schemes), you can record the \textbf{Free / Bonus Packs} separately. This ensures your stock count is 100\% accurate without messing up your purchase cost valuation.
}

\featurecard{6. Physical Stock Audit Worksheet}{Monthly Shelf Count}{
A built-in digital worksheet lets you walk down your aisles, count shelf stock, and enter actual counts. The system flags any discrepancy (breakages, damaged bottles, shrinkage) and logs the adjustment with a reason for permanent record-keeping.
}

\benefitbox{
Cuts financial losses from expired drugs, ensures strict DDA regulatory compliance, helps new staff find medicines immediately, and keeps shelf counts 100\% accurate.
}

\section{Customer Credit (\textit{Udharo / Khata}) Ledger}

In community pharmacies, trusted regular customers often buy medicines on credit (*Udharo*). Instead of writing in paper copy notebooks where pages tear and calculations get lost, your software provides a modern, automated **Digital Khata**.

\featurecard{1. Complete Customer Khata Directory}{Digital Ledger}{
Create customer profiles with their name, mobile phone number, home address, and a customized maximum credit limit (e.g., Rs. 5,000 max credit).
}

\featurecard{2. Automatic Debt Posting at POS Counter}{Zero Double-Entry}{
When a regular customer purchases medicines on credit, the cashier simply selects **Payment Method: Credit** and picks the customer's name. The bill completes immediately, and the total amount is automatically added to that customer's digital ledger debt. You never have to manually write in a paper notebook again.
}

\featurecard{3. Fast Payment Settlements (Full or Partial)}{Simple Balance Clearing}{
When the customer comes to clear their balance:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Open the 	extbf{Customer Credit} view and find their name.
    \item See their exact total outstanding balance.
    \item Click 	extbf{Settle Payment}, enter the amount paid (full or partial payment), choose Cash or QR Code, and save.
    \item Their balance updates instantly, and an official payment receipt is generated.
\end{itemize}
}

\featurecard{4. Clear Customer Account Statements}{Printable Account History}{
Print or show an itemized ledger statement showing every bill taken on credit, every payment made, dates, bill numbers, and the running balance. This eliminates customer disputes over bill amounts.
}

\benefitbox{
Eliminates lost credit notebooks, prevents customers from exceeding their credit limits, avoids awkward calculation arguments, and speeds up debt collection.
}

\section{Hassle-Free Sales Returns \& Credit Notes}

Customers occasionally return unopened medicines (e.g., when a patient's prescription is changed by their doctor). Your software handles this cleanly and accurately.

\featurecard{1. Quick Invoice-Linked or Ad-Hoc Returns}{Simple Return Entry}{
Search the customer's original bill number to view the exact items they purchased and return specific quantities, or process an ad-hoc counter return if they brought the medicine without an invoice.
}

\featurecard{2. Automatic Inventory Restocking}{Accurate Stock Return}{
Returned medicines are automatically restocked back into your live inventory with their original batch number and pack/unit counts.
}

\featurecard{3. Proper Credit (*Udharo*) Reversal}{No Cash Drawer Errors}{
If a customer returns medicine that was purchased on credit and has not yet been paid for, the system automatically **reduces their outstanding debt in their Khata** instead of taking physical cash out of your cash drawer.
}

\featurecard{4. Official Credit Notes \& 13\% VAT Adjustment}{Tax Compliant}{
Generates a sequentially numbered Credit Note and automatically reverses the 13\% VAT collected on taxable items, keeping your tax records in perfect order.
}

\stepbox{Workflow 1: How to Bill a Customer at Counter}{
1. Click 	extbf{POS Billing} in the left menu.\\
2. Type the medicine name or scan the barcode $\rightarrow$ click on the medicine.\\
3. Enter the quantity (packs or loose tablets) $\rightarrow$ click Add to Cart.\\
4. If giving a discount, enter the flat amount or percentage.\\
5. Select payment mode: **Cash**, **Fonepay / QR**, **Card**, or **Credit**.\\
6. Click **Complete Sale \& Print** $\rightarrow$ receipt prints automatically.
}

\stepbox{Workflow 2: How to Sell Medicine on Customer Credit (Udharo)}{
1. At POS Billing, add medicines to the cart.\\
2. Under Customer, select an existing customer or click 	extbf{Add New Customer}.\\
3. Select Payment Method: **Credit**.\\
4. Click 	extbf{Complete Sale} $\rightarrow$ bill prints, and debt is automatically posted to their Khata.
}

\stepbox{Workflow 3: How to Settle a Customer Credit Payment}{
1. Click 	extbf{Customer Credit} in the left menu.\\
2. Search customer by name or phone number $\rightarrow$ see their current balance.\\
3. Click 	extbf{Settle Payment} $\rightarrow$ enter amount paid (Cash or QR) $\rightarrow$ save.\\
4. Balance updates instantly, and a payment receipt is generated.
}

\stepbox{Workflow 4: How to Handle a Doctor Clinic Patient}{
1. Arriving patient: Click 	extbf{Clinic} $\rightarrow$ click 	extbf{Register Patient} $\rightarrow$ enter name, age, and phone.\\
2. Check vitals (BP, Pulse, Temp, Weight) $\rightarrow$ assign patient to Doctor's Queue.\\
3. Doctor opens patient, writes diagnosis, selects prescribed medicines, and clicks **Save**.\\
4. Front counter cashier opens POS $\rightarrow$ clicks **Load Clinic Prescription** $\rightarrow$ bills customer with zero typing.
}

\stepbox{Workflow 5: End-of-Day Cash Balancing Routine}{
1. At closing time, open 	extbf{Reports} $\rightarrow$ 	extbf{Daily Summary}.\\
2. Read the **Total Cash Collected (NPR)**.\\
3. Count the physical cash in your drawer $\rightarrow$ ensure it matches the report.\\
4. Log out of the counter terminal.
}
